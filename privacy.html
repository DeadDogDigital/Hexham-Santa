Privacy Notice

Hexham Christmas · Last updated 4 November 2026

This notice explains how Hexham Christmas handles personal information when you visit our website, book or attend the Hexham Santa Experience, contact us, or use the Santa’s Treats booklet.

1. Who we are

For the activities described in this notice, the data controller is:

Hexham Christmas
3 St Matthews Road
Hexham
Northumberland
NE46 2AZ

Privacy enquiries: info@hexhamchristmas.co.uk.

2. The information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Booking and contact details, such as the booking holder’s name, email address, telephone number and booking reference.
  • Information needed to organise your visit, such as the number of children and adults attending, children’s names and ages, session details, and information you choose to provide about a child’s interests or requirements.
  • Messages and correspondence, including questions, requests and feedback.
  • Information about Santa’s Treats booklets, including unique references and whether offers have been redeemed.
  • Photographs or other material where submitted to us or taken as part of an activity, where applicable.
  • Basic technical information generated when you use our websites, such as device, browser and log information, where collected by our website and hosting services.

Please do not include unnecessary sensitive or medical information in booking notes. If you need to tell us something important to make a visit safe or accessible, contact us directly.

3. How we use personal information

We use information to process and manage bookings, payments, changes and cancellations; prepare for and provide your visit; make reasonable arrangements where you tell us about access or participation requirements; issue and administer Santa’s Treats booklets and record redemptions; respond to enquiries and provide important booking information; manage event operations and prevent misuse; and meet legal, accounting, insurance and regulatory obligations.

4. Our lawful bases

Depending on the purpose, we rely on:

  • Contract: to manage a booking and provide the experience or related services.
  • Legitimate interests: to administer and improve the event, respond to enquiries, maintain appropriate records, protect our services and manage operations, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose information to comply with the law.
  • Consent: where we ask permission for a particular use, such as optional use of a photograph or direct marketing where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Children’s information and photographs

Bookings should be made by an adult. We ask the booking holder to provide only information about children reasonably needed to organise and deliver the visit. Please do not provide information about a child unless you are their parent, guardian or otherwise authorised to do so.

Photographs supplied to us or taken during the experience will be handled for the purpose explained when collected. We will seek any permission required before using identifiable photographs for publicity or promotional purposes. Contact us with any question or concern about a photograph.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information needed with service providers who help us operate the event and its systems, including:

  • Ticket Tailor, for ticketing and booking administration;
  • payment service providers used by the ticketing process, to handle payments;
  • Supabase, for database, authentication and related application services;
  • Netlify, for website hosting and server-side functions;
  • GiftLocal and participating local businesses, where necessary to issue, validate or redeem a Santa’s Treats offer. Businesses should only receive information needed to validate an offer, not your full booking details.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect people’s safety, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Service providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, appropriate safeguards will be used as required by data protection law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this notice, including managing the event, handling queries or complaints, and meeting legal, accounting and insurance requirements. Retention periods differ depending on the type of record. When information is no longer needed, we will securely delete it or anonymise it.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information; ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information; ask us to erase information or restrict its use; object to processing based on legitimate interests; request a copy of information you provided in a portable format where the right applies; and withdraw consent where we rely on consent.

To exercise a right, email info@hexhamchristmas.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

9. Website cookies and similar technologies

Our website and services embedded in or linked from it may use cookies or similar technologies needed to operate the site or provide third-party services. Optional analytics or marketing cookies should only be used in accordance with applicable consent requirements. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

10. Keeping information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No method of online transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or the way we use information changes. The latest version will be published here with its revision date.

Questions? Contact Hexham Christmas at info@hexhamchristmas.co.uk or write to 3 St Matthews Road, Hexham, Northumberland, NE46 2AZ.
